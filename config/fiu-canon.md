THE HEAD COACH (the person playing; his in-game name comes from the game data)
- Miami native, lifelong Miami Dolphins fan; knows South Florida football culture and the talent in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach.
- Heisman Trophy-winning quarterback at the University of Florida.
- Drafted by the New York Giants, traded to the Miami Dolphins before playing a game for New York; became Miami's starting quarterback and lived out his childhood dream until leg and knee injuries ended his career.
- Coaching path: Dolphins quarterbacks coach (two seasons); back at Florida under Dan Mullen, quarterbacks coach then offensive coordinator; offensive coordinator at LSU under Ed Orgeron, including the 2019 national title season, where (in this fiction) he worked with Joe Burrow and Ja'Marr Chase; then the Cincinnati Bengals (reached the Super Bowl and lost); then the Buffalo Bills.
- FIU is his first head coaching job. He chose it over bigger names: financially comfortable, wants to build something lasting at home, not a stepping stone. Guiding belief: the best opportunity is the one worth building.
- Reputation: quarterback developer and offensive architect, but at FIU he is building from the trenches.

PROGRAM IDENTITY
- Build in the trenches first (offensive and defensive lines). Physical, downhill run game; ball security; quick passing; selective play-action and deep shots. Disciplined, aggressive defense schemed around the real roster.
- Recruit overlooked regional speed from South Florida; affordable one- and two-star prospects developed patiently; higher-rated players only when realistic; the transfer portal used selectively, mainly for veteran linemen.
- Long-term goal: a dual-threat quarterback who can really pass.
- Starting point: about a one-star program, team overall 74, few Dynasty Points. Standout young running back Devonte Lyons; quarterback JJ Kohl has a big arm but inconsistent deep accuracy.

THE LEAGUE STRUCTURE (custom house rules, fictional)
- A three-tier promotion/relegation pyramid: American (top tier, 14 teams) > Sun Belt (12) > Conference USA (12, bottom tier). Charlotte opens in Conference USA, alongside FIU: the commissioner judged them a bottom feeder not ready even for the Sun Belt, so they start at the bottom of the pyramid just like FIU. Promotion runs Conference USA top two up to the Sun Belt, Sun Belt top two up to the American.
- Each year, by regular-season conference winning percentage: the top two of the Sun Belt and of Conference USA move up; the bottom two of the American and of the Sun Belt move down. No exceptions, FIU included. A conference championship is its own achievement and does not change promotion.
- Possible far-future storylines, none promised: an American title, national relevance, an ACC invitation, offers from Florida or the Dolphins. Nothing is predetermined; never predict or announce these as coming.

- Keep career offers, realignment and big storylines organic: discuss them only as speculation, never as fact.
